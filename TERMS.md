## 1. What the app does

Battery Alert %1 monitors your battery level on your device. When the battery reaches exactly 1% and you are not charging, it shows a full-screen red pop-up, plays a sound, and vibrates — once per low-battery episode — so you can plug in the charger before the phone shuts down.
