Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026 · App: Battery Alert %1, version 1.4 · Package: com.bmo.sarjuyari

Overview

Battery Alert %1 monitors your battery level on your device. When the battery reaches exactly 1% and you are not charging, it shows a full-screen red pop-up, plays a sound, and vibrates so you can plug in the charger before the phone shuts down. This policy explains what the app accesses, what it stores, and what it never does with your data.

The short version: Battery Alert %1 collects nothing. It has no account, no analytics, and no ads. Nothing leaves your phone.

Data we do not collect

Battery Alert %1 does not collect, transmit, store on a server, sell, or share any personal data, including:

  • Your phone number, contacts, or identity
  • Your battery history or usage patterns
  • Your location, device identifiers, or usage statistics

There is no server, no account system, and no third-party SDK in the app.

Data processed on your device

To do its job, the app reads the following only on your device:

  • Battery level and charging state — to know when the battery hits 1% and whether you are charging.
  • Your settings — such as whether protection is started, stored locally on your device.

No personal data is stored. Battery monitoring and settings all stay on your phone.

Permissions and why

The app requests only the permissions it needs to function: running a foreground service to keep monitoring, sending notifications, vibrating, displaying over other apps (for the pop-up, including on the lock screen), restarting after reboot, and waking the screen for the alert. See the Permissions section on the home page for the full list.

Third parties

Battery Alert %1 shares nothing with third parties, because it sends nothing anywhere. The app's website and GitHub repository are public pages; visiting them is subject to GitHub's own privacy policy, which is outside this policy.

Security

All processing happens locally on your device, protected by your device's own lock screen and operating system. The app's source code is public (MIT licensed), so anyone can read exactly what it does.

Children's privacy

Battery Alert %1 is not directed at children, and it collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version on this page applies. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue in the repository: github.com/lunrofi/battery-alert/issues.
