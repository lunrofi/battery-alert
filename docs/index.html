Everything it does

One small app, no heavy libraries, plain Java. Every feature below is in version 1.4.

🔋

Triggers at exactly 1%

Warns only when the battery hits 1% — and only when you are not charging.

🚨

Full-screen red pop-up

A big red warning even on the lock screen, so you cannot miss it.

🔔

Sound + vibration

A normal notification sound plus vibration. No alarm, no panic siren.

🗘

Once per episode

Warns only once per low-battery episode. No repeat spam.

🔌

Stops when you plug in

The moment the charger is connected, the warning stops automatically.

🔄

Foreground service

Keeps monitoring in the background and auto-restarts if swiped away.

🚀

Auto-start after reboot

Protection restarts by itself when the phone boots.

Modern dark UI

Dark interface with a live battery percentage, in English.

How it works

Four steps, then your phone will never die on you without warning.

1

Install the APK

Download One Percent Alert 1.4 from Releases and install it.

2

Open the app

Open One Percent Alert on your phone.

3

Tap Start Protection

One tap starts the battery monitor.

4

Allow two permissions

Allow notifications and "Display over other apps" when asked. For best reliability, exclude the app from battery optimization.

BATTERY 1%!

Your phone is about to shut down. Plug in the charger now!

Tap OK to dismiss that pop-up. It warns only once per episode.

Questions, answered

Does it warn at other battery levels?

No. It triggers at exactly 1% battery, and only when you are not charging. That is the last chance before the phone shuts down.

Does it keep warning over and over?

No. It warns only once per low-battery episode, with no repeat spam, and it stops automatically when you plug in.

Which Android versions work?

Android 8.0 and newer. The app is plain Java with no heavy libraries, about 37 KB.

Is it on Google Play?

Not yet. Right now it is distributed from GitHub.

How do I get warned at 1% battery on Android?

Install the APK from Releases, open One Percent Alert, tap Start Protection, and allow notifications and display over other apps when asked. For best reliability, exclude the app from battery optimization.
